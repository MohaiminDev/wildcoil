# Product Sense

## Product Thesis From Repo
- `Rift Road` should feel like a new 1990s arcade road adventure, not a remake or borrowed franchise.
- The prototype should prove fast side-scrolling action, readable combat, original jungle-road ruins staging, and a macOS package.
- The current milestone is a presentable Stage 1 team-review prototype.

## Current Audience Assumption
- `docs/game_spec.md` names players who want modern beat-'em-up immediacy, readable melee combat, and a distinctive pulp-tech wilderness identity without live-service bloat.

## Current Proof Points
- Godot runtime exists under `src/wildcoil`.
- Automated tests validate runtime files, campaign content, content rules, and headless launch.
- Packaging script exists for macOS export.

## Product Risks
- Originality drift is tracked in `docs/risk_register.md`.
- Combat feel and readability risks are tracked in `docs/risk_register.md` and `to-do.md`.
- Packaging/notarization risk is tracked in `docs/macos_build_and_distribution.md` and `docs/risk_register.md`.
