# Rift Road Market-Readiness Audit - 2026-05-10

## Objective Restated

Make `Rift Road: Beasts of the Afterglow` meaningfully marketable by proving these deliverables:

1. A playable Stage 1 vertical slice.
2. A production-deployable macOS build path.
3. Real launched-game screenshot and playtest evidence.
4. Current validation results.
5. A candid market-readiness assessment.
6. Evidence that supports public playtest or release-candidate status before calling the goal complete.
7. Player love or commercial viability backed by player or market evidence before claiming the game can earn money.

The 2026-05-11 replacement drafts in `docs/game_spec.md` and `docs/game-story.md` sharpen the internal bar: the first success condition is a Stage 1 slice that feels good, looks alive, and is satisfying to play on the target M1 iMac. Market-facing decisions come after that fun/feel proof, not before it.

2026-05-13 scope clarification: the immediate operating target is source-run local playability on the primary computer. `bash scripts/check_local_playability.sh` is the local end-to-end source-run gate; Developer ID signing/notarization, Gatekeeper acceptance, and second-machine/source-install proof remain downloadable app distribution gates, but they are not a source-run blocker.

## Completion Decision

The active goal is not complete.

The current build is a stronger internal vertical slice than before: it launches from an exported macOS app, has a rebuilt package, starts Stage 1 through real input, has a more readable opening wave, has a less placeholder-like title screen, has data-driven opening story panels and in-stage Raya/Nika barks with a launched opening-story capture, has a centered compact Stage 1 intro strap with non-ellipsized `Free the transport cages` display copy, has a slimmer top HUD footprint, has a one-line combat objective rail, clears stale wave notice text before the post-intro combat proof, has a repeatable post-intro launched combat capture after the strap fades, has data-driven health and luma/meter pickups with a launched pickup clarity capture, has a road-collapse set piece and exported-app capture between the opening fight and service-lane wave, has Brask intro/phase/escape story beats with a launched Brask intro capture, has less placeholder-like hero-select cards plus a canted hero-select header/ribbon, has framed Stage Clear and Game Over/retry result screens, has a Stage Clear rank/score/luma/health summary, has repeatable post-retry launched gameplay proof, and passes the current automated validation suite. The 2026-05-11 spec/story update still raises the next bar from incremental presentation polish to externally verified Stage 1 feel validation. That is meaningful progress toward marketability, but it is not enough to claim production readiness, player love, or commercial viability.
